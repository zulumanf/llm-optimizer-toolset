# 90-day delivery template (spec 131)

The delivery skeleton for a retained client. Facts and dependencies control the
order; the calendar does not. Worked example: `first-client-activation-pack.md`.

## Plan shape (5–8 tasks, no more)
| Week | Theme | Typical tasks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify and prepare | Public audit of every page the answers cite (record before-states); baseline frozen; exclusivity active; onboarding email; first approval request |
| 2 | Public profiles | Client's team applies field-level changes from our instructions; approvals recorded |
| 3 | Owned site | Identity line live; factual evidence pages for confirmed priorities (provenance-tagged claims only) |
| 4 | Verify and measure | Public re-check of every change; change log complete; Month 1 review; early check planned |
| 5–9 | Iterate | Remaining high-confidence items; blockers cleared or declined |
| 9–10 | Final remeasurement | Same instrument; comparability graded; 90-day review prepared |
| 10–12 | Review and decide | Renewal review; renew or offboard |

Every task carries: what, why, evidence, owner, client dependency, approval, done-when, measurement link. No hours unless useful.

## Measurement beats
- BASELINE: frozen Day 0 over the pre-sale run (corrected evidence)
- EARLY CHECK: ~day 30, diagnostic; never reported as progress
- FINAL: ~day 66, before the renewal review (canonical slots today are day 45 and end−10; record the final run into the final slot when it is done — the slot date is a due date, not a lock)
- RENEWAL REVIEW: ~day 70–73, with the final measurement in hand
- TERM END: day 90

No weekly reruns. A changed instrument is recorded non-comparable.

## What counts as progress (three ledgers, never a composite)
1. Delivery: changes live with before/after; approvals resolved; blockers cleared
2. Measurement: recommendations a → b of N; distinct questions; rival count; questions gained/lost; category movement
3. Representation: own site cited or not; one identity named or not; dominant source

## Weekly update (composed by the OS; sent by the founder)
DONE · IN PROGRESS · NEED FROM YOU · MEASUREMENT · NEXT. If nothing material happened, it says so.

## Month 1 review
What we learned · what we changed · what remains · what we are testing · what we need · when we measure next. Portal summary plus a one-page email.

## 90-day review
Starting point · what we learned · what we changed · what happened · comparability/methodology · what we can attribute · what we cannot attribute · what remains · recommended next 90 days · renew/complete.

## Don't list (default)
No redesign; no generic pages; no chasing every cited domain; no unsupported area claims; no public wording without approval; no instrument changes; no causality; no rerun hunting; no passwords; no adjacent-service creep.

## Scope language (default)
In: measurement, diagnosis, identity/profile/owned-page evidence changes, coordination, monitoring, remeasurement, reporting. Out unless agreed: redesign, SEO retainer, social, ads, CRM, PR, custom development, photography, listing marketing, other markets.
