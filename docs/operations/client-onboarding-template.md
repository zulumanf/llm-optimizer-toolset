# Client onboarding template (spec 131)

Reusable for any retained client. The worked example is
`first-client-activation-pack.md` (Ryan Ogle / Blu House). Rule: ask only what
we cannot obtain ourselves AND that materially changes the work. Maximum five
questions; three is the norm.

## Before writing the email, fill from canonical records
- Business, lead agent, brokerage, verified aliases (company record, RealTrends)
- Primary contact and role (prospect_contacts)
- Market node, segment, service category (launch)
- Baseline: provider, questions, repetitions, valid answers, client count and distinct questions, top rival's count (frozen package or the read-only preview)
- Sources the recommending answers cite; whether the client's own site is cited
- Gap concentration (neighborhood / audience / property type) and the rival's leading questions
- What the client said they want (reply ledger)

## The three default questions
1. Priorities: which two or three neighborhoods (or segments) matter most for the term, and is that sellers, buyers, or both? Name the areas the answers already surface so the client confirms or corrects.
2. Comparison set: "We measure you against <rival> because the answers recommend them most. Anyone else, or is that right?" (Must be answered before the baseline is frozen if the answer adds a competitor.)
3. Access: who on the client's side can edit the owned site and each cited profile; ask for an editor invite on the site; profiles are edited by the client's team from our field-by-field instructions.

State defaults instead of asking: approvals go to the signer unless delegated; updates weekly by email; no passwords ever.

## Email skeleton
Subject: <Business>, next <term> days

- One-sentence thanks; term start; agreement and payment confirmed; "I have started."
- What we do this week without them: lock the starting point (numbers with denominators), audit the pages the answers draw from, findings by <day>.
- The three questions, numbered, reply-by-email.
- Two ground rules: public changes sent for approval with current/proposed/why; editor invites or client-applied changes, never passwords.
- Cadence: weekly update; same questions re-asked mid-term and before the end, counted the same way; we say what we can and cannot attribute.
- Optional 20-minute call offered, not required.

## Kickoff call rule
Default no. Offer it for the client's preference. If held: the three questions and the approval flow; 20 minutes maximum.

## Day 0 after the email
Record "update sent" on the Engagement page so the cadence signal starts; record each answer as a context item with the right provenance (publicly_observed vs client_confirmed vs client_priority); access items start `requested`.
