## Reading the table

- **Nothing needs a rebuild.** Every "Missing" capability composes over
  existing spines (approvals, audit, evidence, versioning, workflow
  engine). The recorded architecture decision against rewrites stands.
- **The single blocking theme is authorization depth** (rows 2, 16, 21).
  Until `visibleProjectIds`/`assertCanWrite` are enforced and the export
  routes scoped, no client-facing capability may ship and even internal
  multi-operator use is unsafe.
- **The two genuinely new builds** are the exclusivity/conflict engine
  (row 3) and campaigns/experiments (row 11) — both deliberately thin,
  both reusing existing idioms.
